# Governance

The Evidence Commons RFC series is originated by **Arjay Asadi** and published through **SubstrateX®**.

The standards process is intentionally separable from any one Commons operator, interface, deployment, storage vendor, or reference implementation.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers SHALL:

- preserve RFC and schema history;
- publish incompatible semantic changes explicitly;
- keep conformance distinct from certification;
- disclose normative versus informative material;
- retain rejected and superseded decisions where material to interoperability;
- avoid treating one deployment topology as mandatory;
- avoid creating evidence authority in the Commons that was absent upstream.

## Working-group participation

Implementers, researchers, auditors, standards engineers, privacy specialists, preservation specialists, and repository operators may submit issues, change proposals, test fixtures, mappings, and interoperability reports.

## Certification boundary

Passing public tests or implementing an ECS RFC does not constitute official SubstrateX certification. Certification marks remain separately controlled.
