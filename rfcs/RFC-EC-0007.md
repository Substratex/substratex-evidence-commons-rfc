# RFC-EC-0007 — Aggregation

**Status:** Draft  
**Target:** ECS 1.0  
**Namespace:** `urn:substratex:evidence-commons:1.0:`  
**Originator:** Arjay Asadi  
**Publisher:** SubstrateX®


## 1. Aggregation contract

Every standards-conformant aggregate SHALL be governed by a versioned Aggregation Contract or equivalent machine-readable record.

It MUST define:

- purpose;
- population or universe;
- inclusion criteria;
- exclusion criteria;
- comparability requirements;
- accepted artifact and publication classes;
- accepted disclosure postures;
- method/version constraints;
- date or evidence horizon;
- duplicate handling;
- challenge/supersession handling;
- output measures;
- boundary statement.

## 2. Compatibility

Detailed aggregation MUST NOT include artifacts that fail the declared compatibility requirements.

## 3. Duplicate handling

Replicated or federated copies of the same origin artifact MUST NOT be counted as independent observations.

## 4. Challenge handling

The contract MUST declare whether challenged, disputed, superseded, or withdrawn artifacts are excluded, separately counted, or included with disclosure.

## 5. Transparency

Aggregate outputs SHALL preserve:

- contract identity and version;
- population definition;
- included artifact identities or resolvable membership manifest;
- excluded counts/reasons;
- method versions;
- relevant disclosure limitations;
- date/horizon;
- known challenges.

## 6. Generality boundary

Aggregation does not by itself establish:

- scientific generality;
- causation;
- population validity outside the declared sample;
- model safety;
- legal liability;
- truth.

A conformant aggregate SHALL NOT imply these without separate authority.
