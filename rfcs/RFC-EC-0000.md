# RFC-EC-0000 — RFC Process and Status Model

**Status:** Draft  
**Target:** ECS 1.0  
**Namespace:** `urn:substratex:evidence-commons:1.0:`  
**Originator:** Arjay Asadi  
**Publisher:** SubstrateX®


## 1. Purpose

This RFC defines the lifecycle and change-control process for Evidence Commons standards-track documents.

## 2. Normative statuses

An ECS RFC SHALL have one of:

`Draft`, `Review`, `Candidate`, `Accepted`, `Superseded`, `Withdrawn`.

A status transition MUST be recorded in repository history.

## 3. Normative language

Capitalized **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, and **MAY** are normative.

## 4. Acceptance

Major interchange or federation RFCs MUST NOT become Accepted solely on the basis of schema validation. Candidate evidence SHOULD include at least two interoperable implementations.

## 5. Compatibility

A change that alters object identity, admission semantics, disclosure meaning, comparability, challenge state, preservation history, or federation semantics MUST be treated as normative.

## 6. Versioning

ECS uses semantic standards versions:

`ECS <major>.<minor>`

Major versions may change compatibility. Minor versions may add backward-compatible fields, vocabularies, or clarifications.
