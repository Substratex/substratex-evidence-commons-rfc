# Namespace

ECS 1.0 reserves the namespace:

```text
urn:substratex:evidence-commons:1.0:
```

Examples:

```text
urn:substratex:evidence-commons:1.0:artifact
urn:substratex:evidence-commons:1.0:admission
urn:substratex:evidence-commons:1.0:comparison
urn:substratex:evidence-commons:1.0:aggregation
urn:substratex:evidence-commons:1.0:federation-transfer
urn:substratex:evidence-commons:1.0:conformance-report
```

Namespaces identify vocabulary and interchange semantics. They do not create ownership of third-party evidence or confer certification.
