# Glossary

**Commons Artifact** — A portable evidence-bearing package or evidence reference admitted to an Evidence Commons under a declared admission record.

**Commons Node** — A system that stores, indexes, transmits, validates, governs, or preserves Commons Artifacts. A node need not be public.

**Custodian** — A party or system currently responsible for preserving or transmitting an artifact copy.

**Admission Record** — The machine-readable decision describing whether and under what restrictions a submitted artifact entered a Commons.

**Disclosure Posture** — The declared extent to which source material is available for independent inspection.

**Publication Class** — The access/publication state under which an artifact is exposed by a Commons.

**Comparability Assessment** — A declared evaluation of whether two or more artifacts are sufficiently compatible for a specified comparison purpose.

**Aggregation Contract** — A versioned specification of inclusion, exclusion, compatibility, population, methods, and disclosures governing an aggregate result.

**Challenge** — A preserved objection to an artifact, finding, method, conformance state, disclosure, comparison, or aggregate.

**Counterevidence** — Evidence submitted in support of or opposition to a challenge or prior artifact.

**Supersession** — A non-destructive relationship identifying a newer artifact, decision, or version that replaces an earlier one for a declared purpose.

**Withdrawal** — A preserved state indicating that an artifact should no longer be actively published or relied upon under the identified Commons context. Withdrawal does not erase historical identity.

**Preservation Event** — A recorded custody, migration, validation, redaction, fixity, challenge, replication, or storage event affecting an artifact's preserved history.

**Federation Transfer** — A declared transfer or replication of an artifact between Commons nodes while retaining identity and custody provenance.

**Conformance** — Satisfaction of declared ECS requirements. Conformance is not certification, truth, validity, safety, or legal sufficiency.
