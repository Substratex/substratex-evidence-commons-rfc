# ECS ↔ Evidence-Governed Computation (EGC)

ECS and EGC govern different layers.

**EGC**: whether a claim is admissible from evidence and how that authority is preserved through projection and egress.

**ECS**: how evidence and its governed metadata move through publication, comparison, challenge, aggregation, preservation, and federation.

Where EGC metadata is carried inside a Commons Artifact, ECS MUST NOT silently:

- convert refused or conditional claims into admitted claims;
- drop required limitations or disclosures;
- broaden scope;
- erase challenge state;
- represent a copied artifact as independent corroboration.
