# ECS ↔ Verifiable Credentials / C2PA

Informative mapping.

Cryptographic credentials or content-provenance manifests may be referenced as integrity, attestation, or custody evidence.

ECS distinguishes:

- **integrity** — whether the declared bytes/representation changed;
- **provenance** — how an artifact was produced or transmitted;
- **attestation** — who asserted a statement;
- **evidence authority** — what the underlying evidence supports.

A valid signature does not make a claim true or scientifically valid.
